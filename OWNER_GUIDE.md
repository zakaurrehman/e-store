# Store owner guide

How to open and run a store on Zendropship. Everything below happens on the platform site (`www.zendropship.io`); your store itself is at its own address, `<your-address>.zendropship.io`.

## Open your store

1. Browse the **Catalogue**. Every product shows **You pay** (the wholesale price charged when your customer orders), **Sells for** (the suggested price in your store) and **You earn**.
2. Click **Create your store** (or **Add to my store** on a product — it is added once your store exists).
3. Choose a store name. The address is suggested from the name and checked as you type; you can change it before you submit. Addresses are 3–30 lower-case letters, digits and hyphens, and some words (such as `admin` or `www`) are reserved.
4. Enter your name, email and a password (10+ characters). Your store is live the moment you submit. Confirm your email from the welcome message.

There is no fee to open a store and nothing to pay upfront.

## Add products

- In the **Catalogue**, click **Add to my store** on anything you want to sell. It appears in your store straight away.
- **Products** in your dashboard lists your store's products with what you pay, what it sells for and what you earn. Use the switch to hide a product without removing it, or the bin to remove it.
- Stock comes from the Zendropship catalogue. When something sells out it shows as sold out in your store automatically. A product withdrawn from the catalogue is marked *Withdrawn by supplier*.

## Set your prices

- **Pricing** sets one rule for the whole store: **Suggested prices** (including the supplier's sale prices) or **My own markup** — a percentage on top of what you pay, rounded to a price ending in .99.
- On **Products**, the price rule link next to each product lets you give it its own markup or a fixed price. The dialog shows your margin before you save and warns you if you would sell at a loss.

## Make it yours

**Design & details**: store name, tagline, logo, homepage image, headline and subheading, an announcement bar, your support email (shown on your contact page and in customer emails) and an accent colour. Changes show in your store as soon as you save.

## Orders

When a customer checks out in your store:

- the order goes to Zendropship fulfilment automatically — you never forward, pack or ship anything;
- your customer gets order, shipping and delivery emails in your store's name;
- you get an email with the order number and total.

**Orders** shows each order's status as it is packed, shipped and delivered, the tracking number once shipped, and your margin: selling price minus wholesale, minus any discount. Shipping and tax go to fulfilment and are not part of your margin. **Customers** lists everyone who has bought from you.

## Getting paid

Card payments through **your own Stripe account** are not available yet. The **Payments** page shows the payment methods your store currently offers and will let you connect Stripe when it is ready. Until then, your margin on every order is recorded in **Orders**.

## Good to know

- Your store and your customers' accounts are separate from other stores: a customer's bag and orders in one store never show in another.
- Zendropship can suspend a store that breaks the terms; a suspended store is hidden from shoppers and can't be changed until it is reopened.
- Custom domains (for example `shop.yourname.com`) are not available yet.
